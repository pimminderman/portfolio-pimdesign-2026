Project snapshot

Sustainability insights beyond the platform

Year

2024

Client

Clarity AI

Tension

Asset managers had sustainability data, but extracting a usable answer meant working through dense tables, scores and company pages.

Decision / changed momentum

We shifted from exposing more data to turning the existing evidence into guided answers—while keeping sources and retrieval steps visible.

Outcome

Complex sustainability information became easier to explore, without asking users to take an AI-generated conclusion on faith.
