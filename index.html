Designing transparency for financial sustainability data

Clarity AI - 2023

Built a reusable transparency panel that breaks down any ESG score into clear drivers, methodologies and underlying data.

Tension

Users needed to understand the evidence behind ESG scores, but tooltips made it difficult to explore historical data, score logs and supporting information while reviewing companies.

Why we solved it

The supporting data was available, but its presentation could not accommodate its depth and complexity. Users needed a way to investigate scores and data points without losing their place in the table.

Outcome

The panel supported a broader transparency initiative that helped increase user satisfaction from 75% to 90%. The interaction pattern was subsequently adopted by five product teams.

Design decisions
  • Replace tooltips with side panels to accommodate richer evidence and historical data.
  • Keep the table visible so users retain context while investigating a score.
  • Allow users to move between table cells and update the panel’s content.
  • Establish a consistent interaction pattern across scores and data points.

Sustainability insights beyond the platform

Clarity AI - 2024

Created an AI-powered experience that helps users explore complex sustainability data and uncover what matters—faster.

Tension

Portfolio and asset managers needed to identify what mattered within large volumes of data, without losing access to the detail behind an insight.

Why we solved it

Similar needs appeared across company, portfolio and fund views. We needed an approach that addressed specific ESG questions while also supporting other domains, including climate and regulatory analysis.

Outcome

Adoption of portfolio-level ESG risk analysis rose from 10% to 12% following the rollout of AI Insights—a 2 percentage-point increase.

Design decisions
  • Surface useful insights while preserving access to supporting detail.
  • Adapt the experience to company, portfolio and fund contexts.
  • Define shared patterns while allowing for domain-specific requirements.
  • Align product teams, domain specialists and engineering around a consistent approach across in-app experiences and reporting.

Automate domain discovery for suppliers

Risk Ledger - 2025

Designed a continuous asset discovery product that gives security teams visibility of their external attack surface and uncovers unknown risks.

Tension

Seventy per cent of suppliers added no more than one asset, raising concerns about the completeness of monitoring coverage.

Why we solved it

Clients needed visibility into additional domains, applications and portals. Automated discovery could expand that visibility, while suppliers needed a straightforward way to confirm which assets belonged to them.

Outcome

Automated discovery accounted for approximately 79–80% of all asset additions over six months. Suppliers verified 21,752 discovered assets, confirming 57% as valid for security scanning and rejecting 43%.

Design decisions
  • Make discovery continuous to reduce reliance on manual supplier submissions.
  • Use company, product and website information to identify potential assets.
  • Balance fast client investigation with straightforward supplier verification.
  • Present assets and security findings in a scannable table.

Risk Ledger

Security Certifications

Risk Ledger - 2026

Automatically populate the most crucial security data: security certifications.

Tension

Thirty per cent of suppliers never responded to information requests, with client teams lacking the capacity to keep chasing them. This left gaps in the supplier network and the evidence available for assessments.

Why we solved it

Research found that suppliers were reluctant to engage because they already had the business, did not want to share information twice, or did not want to create another account. Security certifications offered a practical starting point: 75% of suppliers already published them publicly, and 95% of clients required them. Automatically sourcing this evidence could close information gaps without asking suppliers to repeat the work.

Outcome

Achieved 70% accuracy in certification discovery, with 60% of the 120-client base using the capability to find certifications.

Design decisions
  • Investigate the causes of non-response before defining a solution.
  • Create a systematic solution that can be used for engaged suppliers, prefill answers for suppliers.
  • Automatically source evidence to reduce dependence on manual follow-up.
