Designing transparency for financial sustainability data

Clarity AI

Built a reusable transparency panel that breaks down any ESG score into clear drivers, methodologies and underlying data.

Tension

Investment teams could see an ESG score, but they could not easily see the underlying drivers and data that shaped it.

Why we solved it

Transparency data was available, but 22% of clients still could not find or compare it. We made the data clearer, easier to compare and more accessible.

Outcome

The panel supported a broader transparency initiative that helped lift user satisfaction from 75% to 90%; it was subsequently adopted by 8 product teams.

Design decisions
  • Break each ESG score into clear drivers and underlying data.
  • Keep definitions and methodology in context, rather than hidden in help content.
  • Use comparison-ready patterns so teams can assess companies faster.

Sustainability insights beyond the platform

Clarity AI

Created an AI-powered experience that helps users explore complex sustainability data and uncover what matters—faster.

Tension

Asset managers had sustainability data, but had to dig through tables, scores and company pages to use it. They needed fast answers they could verify.

Why we solved it

Clarity AI had a wealth of data, but customers told us that understanding it still took most of their time. We used AI to aggregate the evidence into faster, decision-ready insights.

Outcome

Made complex sustainability information easier to explore while keeping the underlying evidence visible.

Design decisions
  • Show source evidence and retrieval steps alongside AI-generated answers.
  • Use familiar filters, tables and raw data patterns to build trust.
  • Frame AI as guided exploration, not an opaque recommendation engine.

Automate domain discovery for suppliers

Risk Ledger

Designed a continuous asset discovery product that gives security teams visibility of their external attack surface and uncovers unknown risks.

Tension

Security teams lacked a continuously updated view of supplier-facing assets and the risks those assets introduced.

Why we solved it

Seventy per cent of suppliers added no more than one asset, leaving teams with an incomplete view of their exposure. We automated asset discovery to build a more complete, continuously updated picture.

Outcome

Created a clearer way to monitor supplier-facing assets and surface security issues continuously.

Design decisions
  • Make asset discovery continuous rather than dependent on supplier input.
  • Prioritise previously unknown external assets and the risks they introduce.
  • Use a scan-friendly table so security teams can act on findings quickly.

Risk Ledger

Security Certifications

Risk Ledger

Automatically populate the most crucial security data: security certifications.

Tension

Thirty per cent of suppliers never responded to information requests, with client teams lacking the capacity to keep chasing them. This left gaps in the supplier network and the evidence available for assessments.

Why we solved it

Research identified certifications as a valuable starting point: clients needed evidence for assessments, while suppliers needed to demonstrate that they met requirements for continuing business. Automatically sourcing certification data could help close these gaps without additional follow-up.

Outcome

Achieved 70% accuracy in certification discovery, with 60% of the 120-client base using the capability to find certifications.

Design decisions
  • Investigate the causes of non-response before defining a solution.
  • Prioritise certifications based on client needs, business value and engineering effort.
  • Automatically source evidence to reduce dependence on manual follow-up.
